Study in the UK

What a UK degree gives you

Shorter courses, globally recognised universities and the right to work after you graduate.

1 year

Master's degrees

Most taught Master's courses last 12 months, so you pay less in fees and living costs than for a two-year course.

18 monthsFrom 1 Jan 2027

Graduate visa

Stay and work after graduating. Applications made before 1 January 2027 get 2 years.

3 years

Graduate visa for PhDs

Doctoral graduates keep three years of post-study work rights under the Graduate route.

20 hrs

Part-time work

Degree-level students can usually work 20 hours a week in term and full time in holidays.

£558

Student visa fee

Application fee from 8 April 2026, plus the Immigration Health Surcharge for NHS healthcare.

28 days

Funds rule

Your tuition balance and living costs must sit in your account for 28 days before you apply.

Main intake

September

The widest choice of courses and scholarships. Apply between October and March.

Second intake

January

Many postgraduate courses. Apply between June and October.

Limited

May

Offered by some universities for selected courses. Ask us which ones.

Funding your degree

Scholarships for Indian students

Ways to reduce the cost of a UK degree, from university merit awards to fully funded government schemes.

University scholarships

Most common

Many UK universities offer merit scholarships or tuition fee discounts for international students, including students from India. Awards are usually based on your academic results and are often applied automatically when you get an offer.

  • Typically a reduction on your first-year tuition fee
  • Some need a separate application or essay
  • Deadlines vary by university and intake

Chevening Scholarships

UK government · Master's

Fully funded scholarships for a one-year Master's at any UK university, for future leaders with work experience. They cover tuition, living costs and travel.

  • Usually at least two years of work experience
  • You must return to India for two years after your course
  • Applications open once a year, about a year before your course starts

Commonwealth Scholarships

UK government · Master's and PhD

For students from Commonwealth countries, including India, who want to study in the UK and could not otherwise afford it. Some schemes focus on development-related subjects.

  • Covers tuition, living costs and travel
  • Applications go through a nominating body in India

GREAT Scholarships

British Council and UK universities

Scholarships towards tuition fees for one-year postgraduate courses at participating UK universities, offered in a range of subjects each year.

  • Participating universities and subjects change every year
  • Applied for directly through the university

Eligibility, amounts and deadlines change every year. Always check the official scholarship website before you apply. We will help you find the awards you qualify for and prepare strong applications.

Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Viaduct Global helps students from India apply to universities in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it and how we look after it. If you have any questions, email us at viaductglobal26@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you contact us: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, study level, preferred intake and anything you write in your message.
  • When you become a student we advise: documents needed for your applications and visa, such as academic transcripts, English test results, passport details, financial documents and your statement of purpose.
  • When you use this website: this website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a counselling call.
  • To advise you on courses and universities, prepare your applications and help you prepare your visa documents.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We rely on your consent when you contact us, and on our agreement with you when we provide our services. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with the universities you choose to apply to, and with service providers who help us run our business, such as our email provider. We never sell your personal information.

International transfers

Our team works in the UK and India, so your information may be accessed from both countries. We use secure, access-controlled systems and only share documents with the people who need them.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to two years if you do not go on to use our services. If you do, we keep your records for as long as needed to support your application and meet legal requirements, then delete them securely.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email viaductglobal26@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or, in India, to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Home/Study abroad steps/FAQ

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us on a free call.

Is the counselling really free?

Yes. Your first consultation is free and there is no obligation to continue. We will tell you honestly whether the UK is a good fit for your profile and budget.

How do you choose universities for me?

We look at your academic record, English scores, work experience, budget and career goals, then shortlist courses where you meet the entry requirements and have a realistic chance of a visa.

Do you help with the student visa?

Yes. We help you prepare your funds evidence and documents, check everything line by line and guide you through the application. The final decision is always made by UK Visas and Immigration.

Can you help me find scholarships?

Yes. Many UK universities offer merit scholarships and fee discounts for Indian students, and there are national schemes such as Chevening and Commonwealth Scholarships. We will show you the ones you are eligible for and help with the applications.

Will you support me after I arrive in the UK?

Yes. Our team is based in the UK, so we can help with accommodation, opening a bank account, registering with a doctor and settling in during your first weeks.

When should I start?

Ideally 9 to 12 months before your intake. For September, that means starting between September and December of the year before. Earlier starts give you more choice and more time for scholarships.
