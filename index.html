From first call to first lecture

One counsellor stays with you through every stage. You always know what comes next and which documents we need from you.

  1. Step 1

    Profile check

    We review your marks, English scores, budget and goals honestly, and tell you where you stand.

  2. Step 2

    Course and university shortlist

    A shortlist matched to your profile and career plans, with fees, entry requirements and intake dates side by side.

  3. Step 3

    Applications

    Statement of purpose, references and transcripts prepared and submitted on time.

  4. Step 4

    Offer, deposit and CAS

    We help you compare offers, pay the deposit and get through the university's credibility interview.

  5. Step 5

    Student visa file

    Funds evidence, TB test, documents and the application itself, checked line by line before you submit.

  6. Step 6

    Before you fly

    Accommodation, travel, what to pack and how to settle in during your first weeks in the UK.
